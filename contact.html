Common questions

How do I get a delivery order?

Once freight is paid and the original or e-B/L is surrendered, the destination office issues the DO electronically.

What are your free-time terms?

Standard combined free time is 7 days at origin and 7 at destination. Extended packages are available when you quote.

How do I raise a cargo claim?

Select “Claims” above and include your B/L number, survey report and photos. Claims must be notified within 3 days of delivery.
