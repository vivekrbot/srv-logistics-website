About SRV Logistics & Shipping

Founded in Dubai in 2008, SRV has grown from a single chartered feeder into an integrated container shipping and logistics group operating in 32 countries.

Who we are

A carrier with a forwarder’s heart

SRV Logistics and Shipping Corporation Pvt Ltd combines owned and chartered tonnage, a global agency network and in-house logistics capabilities. Customers get the reliability of a liner operator and the flexibility of a logistics partner.

We specialise in the trades that connect the Arabian Gulf and the Indian subcontinent with the rest of the world: Asia–Europe, the Mediterranean, Africa, the Americas and Oceania. Every service is planned and monitored from our Operations & Management Centre at Chennai Port, and the group is led from our Global Headquarters at Jebel Ali, Dubai.

Sustainability

Decarbonising every voyage

We are committed to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2050, in line with the IMO strategy, with interim targets for 2030 and 2040.

  • Methanol dual-fuel and LNG-ready newbuildings on the Asia–Europe Express
  • Voyage optimisation and just-in-time arrival managed by the Chennai OMC
  • Shore power at Rotterdam, Hamburg and Los Angeles berths
  • Verified emissions reporting for every customer shipment
