Privacy notice

SRV Logistics and Shipping Corporation Pvt Ltd (“SRV”, “we”) respects your privacy. This notice explains how we collect and use personal data when you use this website, request quotes, make bookings, apply for jobs or contact us.

Data we collect

  • Contact details (name, company, email, phone) that you provide in forms
  • Shipment information (references, ports, cargo descriptions)
  • Recruitment information (CV, experience) when you apply for a role
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited

How we use it

We use your data to provide quotes and transport services, process bookings, respond to enquiries, assess job applications, meet customs and security filing obligations, and improve our services. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UAE PDPL, India’s DPDP Act 2023 or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data. Contact privacy@srvlogistics.com.

Data stored in your browser

The quote, booking, tracking-watchlist and portal features of this website store data in your browser’s local storage. You can delete it at any time from myPortal → Profile.

Terms of use & booking conditions

By using this website you agree to these terms. Information on schedules, transit times and prices is provided for guidance only and may change without notice.

  • Quotes are valid for 14 days unless stated otherwise, subject to space and equipment availability, and exclude duties and taxes.
  • Bookings become binding when SRV issues a booking confirmation. Cancellation after equipment release may incur charges.
  • Cut-offs for documentation, VGM and cargo must be met. Late cargo may be rolled to the next sailing.
  • Payment of freight is due before release of the bill of lading or delivery order, unless agreed credit terms apply.

Bill of lading terms (summary)

Carriage is governed by the full terms printed on the SRV bill of lading, which are available on request. In summary:

  • Liability is subject to the Hague-Visby Rules or the applicable mandatory regime.
  • The merchant is responsible for the accuracy of cargo descriptions, weights (VGM) and dangerous-goods declarations.
  • Claims must be notified in writing within 3 days of delivery, and suits brought within 1 year.
  • Demurrage and detention apply after the agreed free time.
  • The governing law and jurisdiction are those stated on the bill of lading.

Cookie policy

This website uses essential browser storage to remember your cookie choice, saved quotes, bookings and portal preferences. Map tiles are provided by Esri (ArcGIS Online basemaps) and fonts by Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when content loads. We do not use advertising cookies.
